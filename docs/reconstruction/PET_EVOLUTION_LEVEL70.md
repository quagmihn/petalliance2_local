# Evolution and level 70 feeding

The stock Pet FEED → MAX action sends the number of potions in a single DWORD field on request 516. The previous handler capped both Pet feeding and Bag use at 99. The owner's log contains repeated rejections for two request sizes above that cutoff, including 160 large EXP potions at Katty level 61. The server now accepts the configured Pet EXP stacks up to the quantity the character actually owns. Other Bag actions retain their limit. Pokémon caps and character-level caps remain enforced, and usage and Pet EXP commit together before the updated inventory and Pet notification.

The installed client starts ordinary evolution through 1247/777 and Super Evolution through 729/728. The backend resolves the selected branch from PetEvoInfo, validates the required level, charges the configured gold, items and optional skin protection, and changes species in place under a row lock. It keeps the Pet UID, EXP, formation slot and ordinary evolution star count. Ordinary evolution releases Relation links that the new form does not support. On Super Evolution, the client explicitly warns that the linked helper Pets are consumed and the resulting main Pet starts at zero stars. The server verifies every configured relation slot, helper ownership and five-star status, and refuses equipped helpers. The base Pet remains the same owned UID. The client receives authoritative Pet data before the ACK, including concrete evolution skills resolved from the configured weighted groups. Skill replacements and dismissals use 1667/1653 and 415/414.

`server/run_all.sh` and its doctor validate level-70 thresholds and report 78 ordinary routes, 16 super routes and the blocked material-form route 20000/1. There are 11 optional skill entries with no matching skill type in their configured pools. Those entries are excluded from the optional skill popup. The 94 enabled branches keep original eligibility; the launcher does not change player records.

At diagnosis, `minh` already had Level 70. Katty UID 51 had Level 61, 1,574,799 EXP and five stars. Its current Relation contains one bound helper. X·Katty requires all nine configured linked helpers at five stars, a level-55 or higher Katty and the configured costs (5,000,000 gold, 50 of item 10 and 50 of item 136). No owned Pet or consumable was taken from the main character during validation.

Waydroid on `monster2_test_db` showed the real FEED/MAX interaction reaching Katty Level 70. The client submitted 99 medium, 10 large and 154 extra-large potions. The final batch committed Level 70 and 3,574,299 EXP before the UI returned to STATUS. The same QA Pet then used the Super Evolution confirmation. The server replied 729/728 with success, showed the X·Katty transition, retained UID 13782, exp and its formation slot, consumed the nine configured five-star Relation Pets, charged 5,000,000 gold and took 50 of each required item. Normal evolution and the optional skill-replacement flow passed on real PostgreSQL. The full suite passed 163 tests.

After the Waydroid host restarted on 2026-11-28, the official launcher resumed on `monster2_db` as `monster2`. HTTP 8511 and TCP 8512 started source `a825b95872eb99a893282a734321e8f862126a97`. That helper is at zero stars. All setup that granted or consumed materials for acceptance was on the isolated QA character.
