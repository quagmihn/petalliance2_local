# Market Shop catalog

The Market Shop tab uses AuctionMgrNew's 492/520 search and 481/471 buy protocol. The former backend returned an empty search result. The first Market milestone listed 607 validated offers with the product identifier, item template ID, character ID, quantity and unit price expected by the APK. The existing 90 Commerce/Mall offers are unchanged.

The local Market includes 384 Skill Books (222 active, 82 passive, 80 special), 172 wardrobe vouchers across five appearance slots, and 51 progression, fragment and skill material offers. General consumables use the visible STYLE → MATERIAL category (17/4), with 17/1 accepted for compatible client variants. The Market uses original silver prices for six existing silver offers and a documented local price schedule for other offers. Furniture remains pending its own ownership protocol.

The server locks the offer and expected balance in the purchase path, consumes the session quote once and uses the shared inventory grant. Unlimited silver applies only to the configured player in the private local profile. Waydroid bought 99 Mindpower books (79,200 silver) one Innocent style voucher (600 silver), 99 medium EXP potions (14,850 silver) and 99 Super Evolution Stones (14,850 silver) on the test account. Screenshots and matching TCP records are in `artifacts/arena-missions-20261127/`. Costume equipment and other product families need separate Android acceptance.

## Pet progression and skill books for Level 70

The current private Market contains 610 offers. SHOP → STYLE → MATERIAL carries all five potions enabled by PetFeedViewMgr, costs resolved from PetAdvanceInfo, PetEvoInfo, PetStarUpInfo, configured Train overflow materials, and skill shards. Valid training materials 131 and 137 now also appear. PetStrengthenInfo carries other legacy references with no ItemInfo rows, and the shipped Train screen reads its gold costs. PetFeedViewMgr removes item 10007 from the potion choices, so the shop carries the five client-enabled potions.

SHOP → SKILL groups books by the linked PetSkillInfo family and retains the source grade and attribute for filtering. This moves 13 passive books from ACTIVE into PASSIVE. The target APK defines 209 active books (tiers 1–5), 95 passive books (tiers 2–4), and 80 special books (tiers 1–5). High-grade Pet-only or innate skills without a compatible book remain their original skill type.

The original selectable pet box (item 248, SelectableAwardInfo 1001) is listed at a private-server price of 5,000 silver. Its third choice grants the real template 10001, which the target Pokémon configuration classifies as material with `useType=1` and orange `rarePoint=5`. The client displays the box as `itemname253`. In the default unfiltered order the box is on page 7 of MATERIAL. Its other two choices retain their original rewards. The Pet is accepted only for compatible orange star steps that explicitly list the same substitute; it cannot join a team, receive Pet EXP, be trained, learn skills, bind a Relation or gain its own stars. Database tests cover purchase, opening the original box, formation rejection, a nonmatching Pet rejection and one successful star grant with the material and stone removed.
