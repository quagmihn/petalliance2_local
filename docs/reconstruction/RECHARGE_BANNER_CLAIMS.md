# Recharge campaign eligibility and reward claims

Local simulated checkout remains the sole payment source. The campaign resolver uses committed local receipts and the configuration's active dates and reward references. Campaign 3 uses the first qualifying normal-package receipt and keeps its eligibility record even when its mail is deleted. Campaign 6 uses a subsequent eligible normal receipt within the local game day and produces a separate mail entry. Mail claim 1054/1106 grants 10 × item 10004, 2 × item 178 and 80 diamonds for the daily campaign. Eligibility and the claim are separate, and the database prevents repeat grants.

The 296/299 tier claim supports configured mode-2 and mode-3 paths. Historical campaign dates and zero-duration campaign 8 remain as shipped. PostgreSQL tests exercise initial purchase, repeated normal purchase, the daily claim and duplicate rejection. Waydroid purchased repeatable package N3 with a simulated 99-cent receipt and received 80 diamonds. The resulting Daily Recharge Reward appeared in Town Mailbox. Tapping the gift triggered 1054/1106 and granted another 80 diamonds, 10 of item 10004 and two of item 178. The gift transitioned to claimed, and PostgreSQL stored the corresponding wallet, inventory and claim state. Cold relogin preserved the claimed mail and diamond balance.
